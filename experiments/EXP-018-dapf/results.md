## Interpretation (what the data support, and what they do not)
- The retrained filling-aware models are **worse scorers than the released checkpoint even without any drop** (−2.3 … −4.0 on ShanghaiTech). The loss shows up before any deadline effect, so the downstream losses of +C1 / DAPF most likely come from this base-accuracy gap, not from the provenance mechanism itself. I did not run a control (e.g. the same recipe without provenance or without augmentation) and did not tune anything, so *why* the FA recipe loses accuracy is untested: the 576-parameter flow must now also model filled poses, but that is a hypothesis.
- A fairer comparison for C1 would be against a retrained *standard* model (85.10 mean over 3 seeds, EXP-019), not the released checkpoint (85.94); this narrows the offline gap by ≈ 0.8 but does not close it (FA-gru 83.65).
- What survives: filling beats skipping (re-confirmed, +4.7 pooled; EXP-010 stands) and the learned GRU filler adds a small but consistent gain over constant velocity (+0.5). DAPF as specified (C1 + C2) beats SKIP but not plain FILL-HOLD; its useful part is C2 alone (+C2 arm).

## Deviations and caveats
- **Top-down group is an approximation** (Amendment 1, point 6): the keep mask removes poses from the finished full-rate EXP-016 poses; the tracker and pose model are not re-run, so all arms see tracks formed from every frame. Gains there are probably optimistic for FILL.
- One training seed per FA model and per predictor (the plan asked for none more); the paired bootstrap resamples videos, not training seeds, so a different FA training seed could move the C1 numbers.
- s = 1 has ≈ 1% dropped frames, so those cells say little (all arms ≈ equal).
- FA models trained on CPU under load with our own training loop (not bit-identical to EXP-001's recipe); 0.67% (ShanghaiTech) / 0.40% (UBnormal) of training windows unusable by the augmentation are skipped (Amendment 2).
- Two driver bugs were found and fixed during the grid (numpy int in the GRU sidecar; empty clip in the GRU filler). Both crashed before writing the affected rows, so no result was produced by the buggy code; tests added.
- GAU-FA was not run (EXP-019 G1 failed). C3 not started.
