## 3. Observations

1. **Realistic low light costs little; there is a cliff below it.** Down to mean luma ≈ 37 (L2, close to the darkest
   real UCF-Crime videos) the loss is ≤ 1.5 AUC (live: 0.06); at L3/L4 it is 8/14 (live 4.5/10.7). In the paper
   variant the L2 loss is attributable to sensor noise (darkness only +0.33, noise −1.81), but this split does **not**
   replicate in the live variant (§2.6), so it is not claimed.
2. **Low light hurts through coverage.** At L2/L3, 84% / 59% of the AUC drop is on frames where nobody is scored any
   more; on frames still covered, the pose-based score barely changes at L2 (−0.24). At L4 poses degrade too
   (co-covered AUC 75.6 → 53.5 on 5,757 frames).
3. **Lowering the gates is not a remedy** at any level (all |Δ| ≤ 0.7, L3 −1.7). Lower thresholds add detections
   (+50% at c 0.05) but few new 24-frame tracks (+2% poses), and in the dark the extra poses land more on normal
   frames (L3 normal-frame coverage 0.465 → 0.487) where STG-NF scores them as anomalous → more false alarms.
4. **Whether enhancement helps depends on the noise, and on the enhancer** — the sign flips:
   CLAHE +2.2/+3.2 with noise (L1/L2) but −1.5 without noise and −0.4 on clean; gamma −1.6 to −2.6 everywhere except
   deep dark (L3 +2.9, n.s.); Zero-DCE++ +2.0 at L2, ≤ 0 elsewhere. At L4 nothing helps.
5. **Resolution is the largest single lever in the realistic range**: 480 → 960 px = +9.5 (L0) / +9.6 (L2) AUC, larger
   than the whole L0 → L3 darkness loss. In deep dark (L3) extra pixels stop helping (+0.3): resolution and light
   interact — pixels only help when there is signal in them.
6. **Surprise, replicated across noise seeds:** at L1/L2 the noisy-dark + CLAHE pipeline (70.06 / 70.19; seed 1:
   69.48) scores *higher* than clean frames without enhancement (68.53), with more anomalous coverage (0.699 vs
   0.674), and CLAHE + 960 px at L2 (72.16) beats clean at 960 (71.73). In the live variant the same ordering holds
   but is small (L2 CLAHE 67.06 vs clean 66.17). Plausible reading: CLAHE equalises local contrast, which helps the
   detector on small people; on clean frames it does not (−0.43), so this is not a general "CLAHE always helps".
7. **Deep dark is different:** at L3 only gamma helps (+2.9 paper, +1.7 live, CIs include 0), resolution does not,
   and resolution does not stack with gamma (L3 E1 + 800 = 62.03 < 63.29).

## 4. Discussion

The pre-registered bet — low light hurts mainly by pushing people under the confidence gates, so re-calibrating the
gates is a free fix (H3, H4) — is **refuted**, but its first half (H2: the damage is coverage) holds. People are lost
because the detector does not respond to them at all, not because they sit just under the threshold. The working
remedies are the ones that change what reaches the detector: local contrast normalisation (CLAHE) under moderate
noise, and more pixels (resolution) wherever there is signal. Their benefits are condition-dependent with sign flips
(Obs. 4–5), and each has a CPU cost on the edge device: that makes a **condition-aware choice of preprocessing and
resolution under a CPU budget** the natural "framework" contribution, and makes the budget (EXP-004, OS tuning) part
of the same decision rather than a separate optimisation.

## 5. Threats to validity

- **Prior work (LITCHECK-2026-11-05):** physics-based synthetic low light for person detection exists (arXiv
  2605.22455, Hong BMVC 2021), so the darkening model is a method, not a contribution. PoseBench reports that input
  resolution does not improve robustness — consistent with H14 (no resolution gain at L3).

- **Synthetic low light.** One sensor model (K = 1000 e⁻, σ_r = 2 e⁻, no gain/ISP, no compression). Real night CCTV
  uses auto-gain, IR and H.264, which change both the noise and the enhancement response. Needs real-dark validation.
- **Noise realisation:** checked with a second seed for the two key contrasts (Addendum B): both replicate, configs
  move ≤ 0.71. Other configs have one realisation.
- **Scoring variant matters:** effect sizes are 1.5–3× smaller in the live variant and the noise-vs-darkness split
  does not replicate there (§2.6). Claims are restricted to findings robust in both.
- **ShanghaiTech only**, 107 test videos; CIs are ±4–5 points per config; paired Δs are tighter but many comparisons
  were made (only H4 had a pre-registered multiplicity correction).
- **Frozen STG-NF trained on AlphaPose poses**; YOLO pose style mismatch stays constant across arms.
- Deviations: E3 moved to MPS (parity ≤ 1 LSB); STG-NF loader bug fix (no effect on previously scored configs,
  verified); gate_sweep rerun for the L3 resolution caches. All in `log.md`.
- Addendum A was added after interim gate results (labelled exploratory; written before any resolution run).
