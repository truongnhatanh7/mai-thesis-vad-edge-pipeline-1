# Key papers (condensed from the owner's KB, 2026-11-04)

Each line: what it does → why it matters. Verify before citing; numbers marked in the KB as unverified are left out.

- **Impact of Thermal Throttling on Long-Term Visual Inference in a CPU-Based Edge Device** (Electronics (MDPI) 9(12):2106 2020; benoitcattin2020-thermal-throttling-rpi4) — https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics9122106
  80 long-run cases (5 CNNs × 4 frameworks × 2 OSes × cooling on/off) on a Raspberry Pi 4B: active cooling prevents throttling and raises throughput up to ~90%; without cooling, ambient 22–36 °C changes throughput by up to 27.7%.
- **Avoiding the Ordering Trap in Systems Performance Measurement** (USENIX ATC 2023 2023; duplyakin2023-ordering-trap) — https://www.usenix.org/conference/atc23/presentation/duplyakin
  2.3M measurements on 1,700+ servers over 9 months show trial order can bias results and flip conclusions (carry-over via caches, tuning state, temperature/throttling); proposes order-dependence tests and the OrderSage tool (randomized order, repetition, reset).
- **Normalizing Flows for Human Pose Anomaly Detection** (ICCV 2023, pp. 13545–13554 2023; hirschorn2023-stg-nf) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.10946
  STG-NF: a ~1K-parameter normalizing flow over tracked human-pose sequences that scores how unlikely a person's motion is. ShanghaiTech 85.9 AUC (unsupervised). Checkpoints ship inside the repo, so it is the leading candidate for the thesis's frozen edge workload (pose detector → tracker → STG-NF on a Pi 5).
- **The Art of Computer Systems Performance Analysis: Techniques for Experimental Design, Measurement, Simulation, and Modeling** (Wiley-Interscience (book), ISBN 978-0-471-50336-1 1991; jain1991-art-of-performance-analysis) — https://www.wiley.com/en-gb/The+Art+of+Computer+Systems+Performance+Analysis%3A+Techniques+for+Experimental+Design%2C+Measurement%2C+Simulation%2C+and+Modeling-p-9780471503361
  Classic systems-performance textbook (Wiley 1991); Part IV (ch. 16–23) teaches 2^k, 2^k r and fractional 2^(k−p) factorial designs, allocation of variation and interaction effects — the methodological backbone for a preprocessing × OS factorial study. Link-only (book).
- **LLVIP: A Visible-infrared Paired Dataset for Low-light Vision** (ICCV 2021 Workshops (arXiv 2108.10831) 2021; jia2021-llvip) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.10831
  Introduces LLVIP: 15,488 time- and space-aligned visible–infrared image pairs (30,976 images) from street-mounted surveillance cameras, mostly in very dark scenes, with pedestrian boxes. The CCTV-viewpoint low-light person benchmark lane C said did not exist.
- **Jointly Optimizing Preprocessing and Inference for DNN-based Visual Analytics** (PVLDB 14(2), 2021 2021; kang2021-smol) — https://doi.org/10.14778/3425879.3425881
  Smol: first measurement study showing decoding/resizing (preprocessing) can dominate end-to-end DNN inference cost and power on modern accelerators; up to 5.9x throughput via low-resolution native data and CPU/GPU-aware pipelining.
- **Turbo: Opportunistic Enhancement for Edge Video Analytics** (ACM SenSys 2022 2022; lu2022-turbo-opportunistic-enhancement) — https://doi.org/10.1145/3560905.3568501
  Uses idle, fragmented edge-GPU cycles to enhance only the frames a given detector finds hard (task-specific discriminator + GAN enhancer + multi-exit scheduler), adding 7.3–11.3% detection accuracy without latency cost — the closest 'enhancement under a latency/resource budget' system.
- **Benchmarking Robustness in Object Detection: Autonomous Driving when Winter is Coming** (arXiv preprint 1907.07484 2019; michaelis2019-benchmarking-robustness-detection) — https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.07484
  Pascal-C / COCO-C / Cityscapes-C: ImageNet-C corruptions applied to detection, with mPC and rPC metrics; standard detectors keep only 30–60% of clean performance; stylized-data training improves robustness.
- **ApproxDet: Content and Contention-Aware Approximate Object Detection for Mobiles** (ACM SenSys 2020 2020; xu2020-approxdet) — https://doi.org/10.1145/3384419.3431159
  Multi-branch Faster R-CNN + tracker whose scheduler predicts latency/accuracy from video content AND co-located resource contention and picks a branch under a latency SLA — the closest system to 'input conditions × system state' but GPU-based and without image enhancement.
- **Beyond Inference: Performance Analysis of DNN Server Overheads for Computer Vision** (ACM/IEEE DAC 2024 2024; abouelhamayed2024-beyond-inference) — https://doi.org/10.1145/3649329.3655960
  End-to-end CV serving study: decompression, resizing, normalization and data movement take up to 56% of end-to-end latency (medium images) and ~80% throughput impact (large images) — preprocessing is a first-class performance cost.
- **Anomaly Detection on the Edge Using Smart Cameras under Low-Light Conditions** (Sensors (MDPI) 24(3):772 2024; abuawwad2024-low-light-edge-anomaly) — https://doi.org/10.3390/s24030772
  Smart-camera pipeline for rural security (farm theft, lone-worker safety): a random-forest classifier routes each frame to no enhancement, RUAS or Zero-DCE++, then a detector runs; tasks are split across a Raspberry Pi and Jetson Nano boards. Owner source 2 — the closest of the owner's sources to 'low-light preprocessing for public-safety edge inference', but the enhancers run on Jetson GPUs and the edge test bed is 40 images.
- **Statistically Rigorous Java Performance Evaluation** (ACM OOPSLA 2007 2007; georges2007-statistically-rigorous-java) — https://doi.org/10.1145/1297027.1297033
  Shows prevalent 'best/mean of N runs' benchmarking can reach wrong conclusions under run-to-run non-determinism; advocates confidence intervals, ANOVA for multi-alternative comparisons, and separate startup vs. steady-state protocols.
- **End-Edge Coordinated Joint Encoding and Neural Enhancement for Low-Light Video Analytics** (IEEE GLOBECOM 2023 2023; he2023-joint-encoding-enhancement-low-light) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.16418
  Camera encodes, edge runs a scalable Zero-DCE enhancer before YOLOv5s; an adaptive controller picks codec QP and enhancer width to meet latency while maximizing low-light detection accuracy.
- **Joint Encoding and Enhancement for Low-Light Video Analytics in Mobile Edge Networks** (IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing 24(4) 2025; he2025-jee-low-light-tmc) — https://doi.org/10.1109/TMC.2024.3514214
  JEE: chooses between encode→decode→enhance and enhance→encode→decode pipelines plus encoding/enhancement configs via online gradient descent (sub-linear dynamic regret); up to +27.32% accuracy and −26.18% delay.
- **Scientific Benchmarking of Parallel Computing Systems: Twelve Ways to Tell the Masses when Reporting Performance Results** (SC 2015 2015; hoefler2015-scientific-benchmarking) — https://doi.org/10.1145/2807591.2807644
  Twelve rules for statistically sound performance reporting (report variability and CIs, don't assume normality, compare with sound tests, choose percentiles deliberately, document all factors), from a survey of 120 HPC papers; ships the LibLSB benchmarking library.
- **Rigorous Benchmarking in Reasonable Time** (ACM ISMM 2013 2013; kalibera2013-rigorous-benchmarking) — https://doi.org/10.1145/2464157.2464160
  Cookbook for deciding how many repetitions to run at each level (build, execution/process, iteration) based on where variance actually arises, and for reporting effect-size confidence intervals — keeps a factorial study affordable.
- **Input-Adaptive Gating of a Dehazing Front-End for On-Device Perception in Smoke-Obscured Environments** (arXiv preprint 2608.30034 (submitted to NeurIPS; not peer-reviewed) 2026; kang2026-gated-dehazing-pi) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30034
  On a Raspberry Pi 4 CPU (TFLite UINT8), a dehazer helps edge extraction only in dense smoke and hurts clear frames; a 10.1 ms dark-channel gate that runs the 469.6 ms dehazer only when needed beats both always-on and never (mean ODS 0.675 vs 0.664 / 0.630) and cuts mean frame time 569 → 321 ms. Pre-empts 'brightness-gated preprocessing' as a stand-alone contribution.
- **Anole: Adapting Diverse Compressed Models for Cross-Scene Prediction on Mobile Devices** (IEEE ICDCS 2024 2024; li2024-anole) — https://doi.org/10.1109/ICDCS60910.2024.00063
  Keeps a pool of compact scene-specific detectors (scenes include day/dawn-dusk/night and weather) and a lightweight classifier that picks the best model per frame on Jetson devices; +4.5% accuracy, 33.1% faster, 45.1% less power than one large model.
- **Understanding Time Variations of DNN Inference in Autonomous Driving** (arXiv 2209.05487 2022; liu2022-dnn-time-variations) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.05487
  Characterizes root causes of DNN inference-time variation (data, I/O, model, runtime, hardware); scene content changes post-processing (NMS) time — correlation 0.98 between detected objects and post-processing time for Faster R-CNN, 0.43 for YOLOv3.
- **Benchmarking Object Detection Robustness against Real-World Corruptions** (International Journal of Computer Vision (IJCV) 2024; liu2024-real-world-corruptions-od) — https://doi.org/10.1007/s11263-024-02096-6
  Builds COCO-C and BDD100K-C with 16 corruptions modelled on real camera-sensor and image-pipeline damage, evaluates 12 detectors; colour-change corruptions hurt most, high clean mAP does not imply robustness, one-stage detectors are more stable across scenarios.
- **Sustainable Edge Vision via Empirically Calibrated DVFS: Eliminating Thermal Throttling on Passively Cooled Hardware** (arXiv preprint 2609.04705 (Sep 2026; not peer-reviewed) 2026; marasini2026-calibrated-dvfs-pi5) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04705
  State-aware, empirically calibrated DVFS scheduler for a passively cooled Raspberry Pi 5 running YOLOv8n: no throttling over 30-minute runs, +6.8 % FPS and −1.9 % J/frame vs a temperature-only reactive baseline; passive envelope closes at ≥ 27 °C ambient. Shows thermals matter for detection on a Pi 5 only without the fan.
- **Low-light VRU detection using channel-attention zero-DCE++ with Raspberry Pi deployment** (Transportation Research Interdisciplinary Perspectives 37:102001 (Elsevier) 2026; megalingam2026-zero-dce-vru-pi5) — https://doi.org/10.1016/j.trip.2026.102001
  Trained, modified Zero-DCE++ (channel attention, 4 curve iterations) in front of YOLOv8n for night-time vulnerable-road-user detection; INT8 on a Raspberry Pi 5 at 8.35 FPS (119.7 ms/frame); mAP50 58.9 → 63.47 on a custom Indian night set. Refutes the lane-A claim that no paper reports learned-enhancer latency on an ARM CPU.
- **Producing Wrong Data Without Doing Anything Obviously Wrong!** (ASPLOS 2009 2009; mytkowicz2009-wrong-data) — https://doi.org/10.1145/1508284.1508275
  Shows that innocuous setup details (UNIX environment size, link order) shift memory layout enough to reverse performance conclusions; none of 133 surveyed papers handled this measurement bias. Fixes: setup randomization and causal analysis.
- **Enhancing Video Analytics Accuracy via Real-time Automated Camera Parameter Tuning** (ACM SenSys 2022 2022; paul2022-camtuner) — https://doi.org/10.1145/3560905.3568527
  CamTuner: SARSA RL agent that adjusts non-automated IP-camera parameters (brightness, contrast, colour, sharpness) as environmental conditions change; +15.9% persons detected in a parking-lot deployment — 'preprocessing at the sensor' driven by conditions.
- **An embedded deep learning framework for real-time violence detection and alert generation** (Scientific Reports 2026; salman2026-embedded-violence-detection) — https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-44939-x
  CNN–GRU violence detector compressed (pruning, INT8 PTQ) and run CPU-only with ONNX Runtime on a Raspberry Pi 5: 38.4 ms/frame, ~26 FPS end-to-end. Owner source 4 — a real public-safety, CPU-only edge pipeline (the 'framework' leg), but with internal inconsistencies, a literature review (Sec2) whose descriptions do not match its cited references, live tests under controlled lighting, and no OS-level tuning.
- **LiteReconfig: Cost and Content Aware Reconfiguration of Video Object Detection Systems for Mobile GPUs** (EuroSys 2022 2022; xu2022-litereconfig) — https://doi.org/10.1145/3492321.3519577
  Follow-up to ApproxDet that accounts for the scheduler's own cost and branch-switching overhead, using a cost-benefit analyzer to decide which content features are worth extracting; reaches 30 fps on Jetson TX2 / 50 fps on AGX Xavier.
- **Edge Video Analytics: A Survey on Applications, Systems and Enabling Techniques** (IEEE Communications Surveys & Tutorials 25(4) 2023; xu2023-edge-video-analytics-survey) — https://doi.org/10.1109/COMST.2023.3323091
  Comprehensive IEEE COMST survey of edge video analytics (EVA): definitions, architectures, application taxonomy (law enforcement, surveillance, traffic), systems, enabling techniques, frameworks and datasets up to Aug 2023 — the map for positioning the thesis.
- **A Combination of Light Pre-trained Convolutional Neural Networks and Long Short-Term Memory for Real-Time Violence Detection in Videos** (International Journal of Technology 14(6):1228–1236 2023; abdullah2023-efficientnet-lstm-violence-pi) — https://doi.org/10.14716/ijtech.v14i6.6655
  EfficientNet-B0 features + LSTM classifier for violence/non-violence on RWF-2000 + RLVS (86.38% accuracy), deployed on a Raspberry Pi 4 with a USB webcam and LED alert. CPU-only Pi, but no on-device FPS/latency reported.
- **A Comprehensive Evaluation of Deep Learning Object Detection Models on Heterogeneous Edge Devices** (arXiv preprint 2024; alqahtani2024-edge-object-detection-benchmark) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.16808
  Accuracy/latency/energy benchmark of YOLOv8, EfficientDet-Lite and SSD on Raspberry Pi 3/4/5 (± Coral TPU, AI HAT+) and Jetson, using a USB power meter.
- **Real-Time Video Analytics: The Killer App for Edge Computing** (IEEE Computer 50(10):58–67 2017; ananthanarayanan2017-killer-app) — https://doi.org/10.1109/MC.2017.3641638
  Position paper (Microsoft Research): large-scale live video analytics — surveillance, traffic, safety — needs a geo-distributed cloud + edge architecture down to the cameras because of latency, bandwidth and compute demands.
- **Ekya: Continuous Learning of Video Analytics Models on Edge Compute Servers** (USENIX NSDI 2022 2022; bhardwaj2022-ekya) — https://www.usenix.org/conference/nsdi22/presentation/bhardwaj
  Jointly schedules live inference and retraining of compressed edge models under data drift (lighting, crowd density) with a 'thief' GPU scheduler and micro-profiler; up to 29% higher accuracy than baselines. Shows drift from conditions costs ~22% accuracy for compressed edge DNNs.
- **Research on Delay Attack and Robustness of Object Detection Models on Edge Devices** (DAI 2025 (3rd Int. Conf. on Data Science, Advanced Algorithms and Intelligent Computing), SciTePress, pp. 119–127 2025; chen2025-delay-attack-edge-detection) — https://www.scitepress.org/publishedPapers/2025/143755/pdf/index.html
  YOLOv5 / YOLO-World / NanoDet under CPU contention (`yes > /dev/null`) on an x86 laptop: taskset core pinning cuts latency SD by ≈40 % and P95 by ≈200 ms; JPEG re-encoding is tested as an input-side defence alongside mAP. The only found study putting an input transform and an OS knob in one design with accuracy and tail latency — weak (x86, adversarial framing, no factorial).
- **Accelerating Anomaly Detection in Real-Time Video Streaming on the Edge** (MSc thesis, Politecnico di Torino (Computer Engineering), company partner Sony Europe B.V. 2025; depascale2025-accelerating-vad-edge-imx500) — https://webthesis.biblio.polito.it/36386/
  MSc thesis (Politecnico di Torino, with Sony Europe): quantized S3D-inspired VAD model (MobileNet 2D features → MoViNet spatio-temporal) for a Raspberry Pi with the Sony IMX500 AI camera; ~4M params, 8.27 GFLOPs, UCF-Crime frame-level AUROC >79%. Accelerator-assisted (on-sensor NPU), not CPU-only.
- **Lightweight CNN–MIL Models for Cross-Domain Video Anomaly Detection: A Reproducible Evaluation Framework** (Informatica 49(36):67–80 2025; gupta2025-lightweight-cnn-mil-cross-domain-vad) — https://doi.org/10.31449/inf.v49i36.12037
  MobileNetV2/ResNet-18 + MIL-ranking weakly-supervised VAD evaluated in- and cross-domain (UCF-Crime, ShanghaiTech, Avenue, Railway CCTV) and under corruptions incl. brightness change; in-domain frame-AUC 79–85%, cross-domain drops up to 15%; MobileNetV2–MIL 28–30 FPS on Jetson Nano. GPU-class edge board; no code released.
- **Deploying a Skeleton-Based Video Anomaly Detection System on Edge Devices for Human Activity Surveillance** (IEEE Embedded Systems Letters 18(3):178–181 2026; huang2026-skeleton-vad-edge-pi5) — https://doi.org/10.1109/LES.2025.3618635
  Full skeleton-based VAD pipeline (YOLOv8n-Pose → BoT-SORT tracking → lightweight anomaly-scoring model) deployed end-to-end on a Raspberry Pi 5. Cited in the owner's lit review as evidence that edge surveillance is a multi-stage pipeline competing for the same CPU.
  Full text read 2026-11-05: 86.2 is the scoring model's AUC (pose source for it not stated; compared against STG-NF etc.); Pi 5 at 640×640 = 3.2 FPS, NCNN 320×320 = 17.7 FPS with "some degradation in object detection quality" but no AUC at 320; YOLO + MOT dominate latency; 6.3 W / 6.07 W.
- **RobustML@Edge: Energy-Latency-Accuracy Trade-offs Under Distribution Shift on Raspberry Pi** (TechRxiv preprint (posted 2025-10-30, not peer-reviewed) 2025; jacob2025-robustml-edge) — https://doi.org/10.36227/techrxiv.176186793.32772120/v1
  Unreviewed single-author preprint: classifiers (MobileNetV3-Small, ResNet-18, TinyCNN) × FP32/INT8 × threads on a Raspberry Pi 5 CPU, evaluated on CIFAR-C / Tiny-ImageNet-C with INA219 energy; claims corruptions change latency and energy. Narrows 'robustness and real-time never reported together on a Pi' — for classification only, no preprocessing, no deadlines.
- **Chameleon: Scalable Adaptation of Video Analytics** (ACM SIGCOMM 2018 2018; jiang2018-chameleon) — https://doi.org/10.1145/3230543.3230574
  Dynamically picks NN configurations (resolution, frame rate, model) per camera, amortizing profiling via temporal/spatial correlation: same accuracy with 30-50% of resources.
- **Optimizing Deep Learning Inference on Multi-Core Laptop CPUs with Scheduling and Thread Affinity Strategies** (ACM Southeast Conference (ACMSE 2026), pp. 276–280, short paper 2026; khan2026-thread-affinity-laptop-cpu) — https://doi.org/10.1145/3746467.3801520
  Short paper: default OS scheduling vs more intra-op threads vs ONNX Runtime thread affinity vs manual pinning vs oversubscription for CNN/transformer classifiers on a laptop CPU; ORT affinity gives 5–8× faster inference, while more threads or uncoordinated pinning can hurt via oversubscription. Confirms the OS arm is studied for CPU inference — no preprocessing, x86.
- **Benchmarking Jetson Edge Devices with an End-to-end Video-based Anomaly Detection System** (arXiv 2307.16834; accepted at Future of Information and Communication Conference (FICC) 2024 2023; pham2023-jetson-benchmark-vad) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.16834
  End-to-end RTFM crime-scene anomaly detection (ResNet50-I3D non-local features, PySlowFast template, Docker) benchmarked on Jetson Nano, AGX Xavier and Orin Nano with/without Torch-TensorRT: 47.56 FPS / 3.11 GB RAM on Orin Nano, 1.55 FPS on Jetson Nano. GPU-accelerated, not CPU-only.
- **Federated Learning for Video Violence Detection: Complementary Roles of Lightweight CNNs and Vision-Language Models for Energy-Efficient Use** (ICTAI 2025 2025; thuau2025-federated-violence-energy) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.07171
  Closest VAD-adjacent energy reporting: zero-shot VLMs vs LoRA-tuned LLaVA-NeXT-Video-7B vs a personalised 3D CNN for federated clip-level violence classification (RWF-2000, RLVS) with explicit energy/CO2e (3D CNN 240 Wh vs federated LoRA 570 Wh). Different task and protocol from frame-level VAD.
- **Low-Cost CNN for Automatic Violence Recognition on Embedded System** (IEEE Access 2022; vieira2022-low-cost-violence-embedded) — https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2022.3155123
  Mobile CNNs adapted for violence vs. non-violence classification (up to 92.05% accuracy) deployed on a Raspberry Pi at ~4 fps with a pre-fight warning system — example of CPU-only public-safety edge inference and its real-time limits.
- **Joint Configuration Adaptation and Bandwidth Allocation for Edge-based Real-time Video Analytics** (IEEE INFOCOM 2020 2020; wang2020-jcab) — https://doi.org/10.1109/INFOCOM41043.2020.9155524
  JCAB: online Lyapunov/Markov-approximation algorithm that jointly picks per-stream frame rate/resolution and shares an uplink, balancing accuracy, energy and long-term latency; the canonical 'joint configuration + resource allocation' edge VA paper.
- **AWStream: Adaptive Wide-Area Streaming Analytics** (ACM SIGCOMM 2018 2018; zhang2018-awstream) — https://doi.org/10.1145/3230543.3230554
  Stream-processing framework where developers declare degradation knobs and the system learns an accuracy–bandwidth Pareto profile offline+online, then adapts the data rate at runtime; sub-second latency with 2–6% accuracy drop.
- **A Lightweight Dual-Branch System for Weakly-Supervised Video Anomaly Detection on Consumer Edge Devices** (arXiv 2410.21991 (v1 Oct 2024, revised Jun 2025; submitted to IEEE Transactions on Consumer Electronics) 2024; jiang2024-rulevad-edge) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.21991
  RuleVAD: implicit CLIP-feature branch for coarse binary VAD + explicit branch (YOLO-World objects → FP-Growth association rules as text prompts) for fine-grained classes; 28.8M params / 36.5 GFLOPs; claims SOTA on XD-Violence (87.48 AP) and UCF-Crime (88.58 AUC) and Jetson Nano deployability. No measured on-device FPS found; GPU-class board.
- **Implementasi CLAHE dan YOLOv11 untuk Pembacaan Pelat Nomor Kendaraan Beresolusi Rendah pada Raspberry Pi 5** (Jurnal Minfo Polgan 15(2):2407–2416 (Indonesian polytechnic journal) 2026; nugroho2026-clahe-yolo11-pi5) — https://doi.org/10.33395/jmp.v15i2.16669
  Low-quality anecdote (weak venue, tiny test sets): on a Raspberry Pi 5, CLAHE costs 5.3–9.2 ms per frame next to 192–300 ms YOLO11n inference. The 300 ms matches Ultralytics' PyTorch figure, not an optimised runtime; with NCNN (≈67 ms) CLAHE would be ≈12–14 % of frame time, not ≈3 %.
- **Live Video Analytics at Scale with Approximation and Delay-Tolerance** (NSDI 2017 2017; zhang2017-videostorm) — https://www.usenix.org/conference/nsdi17/technical-sessions/presentation/zhang
  VideoStorm: offline resource-quality profiles + online scheduler for thousands of video queries; up to 80% better quality and 7x better lag than fair sharing.
- **Detection and Tracking Meet Drones Challenge** (IEEE TPAMI 2021; zhu2021-visdrone) — https://doi.org/10.1109/TPAMI.2021.3119563
  TPAMI paper describing the VisDrone benchmark (drone footage from 14 Chinese cities; image/video detection, SOT, MOT tracks) and results of the ECCV 2018 / ICCV 2019 / ECCV 2020 challenges.
